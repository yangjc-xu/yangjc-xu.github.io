Research

My research focuses on the analysis of time-to-event data. Specifically, I am interested in developing novel statistical methods and theory in semiparametric modeling, data privacy, transfer learning and causal inference, with a particular emphasis on time-to-event outcomes.

On the application side, I apply these methods to data from clinical trials, epidemiological cohort studies, the UK Biobank, and electronic health records (EHR), aiming to generate meaningful scientific insights. As an example, my collaborators and I have published a series of papers evaluating the effectiveness of COVID-19 vaccines.

  • Time-to-event data
  • Semiparametric models
  • Survival analysis
  • Functional data analysis
  • Causal inference
  • EHR data analysis
